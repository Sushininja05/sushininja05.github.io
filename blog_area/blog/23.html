So what's the point of this blog entry then? Well it's mainly just house keeping. I've been mainly posting my Grandman updates to my discord server, as I don't really like posting publically about a game I may or may not be able to complete. I want to complete it obviously, but I can't make promises and it's already been 6 whole years since Grandman has started, that's not a good sign. Happy new year btw lol.
I'm done with an incredibly brutal time of my life and things are looking up, so I'm actually able to work on things again. I recently fixed the art page because it was falling apart, and I might be redoing some of the graphics on my website at a later date. Most importantly, I'm working on Grandman again, so let's talk about that.

Grandman?

I spent the latter half of last year refractoring the enemies, redoing parts of the editor/world map/level loading, adding onto Grandman's moveset, finishing up/redoing levels to actually be fun, redoing the combat systems, allowing enemies to walk up slopes, and fixing up various bugs along the way. I am currently working on the first boss, "Regretsman" (tentative name) who is a sort of like a Zenigata-type character who's trying to find Grandman and lock him up. The story for the game is finalized, Grandman's moveset is *finally* complete to me, and the level editor/other authorial tools are in a stable state that allows me to make the game actually *playable* for once. This means I am actually making **TANGIBLE PROGRESS** now, which is scary, I know.

So what is done and what needs to be done, at least for the Demo? Well I plan to ship the demo with 10 levels from two worlds (the first and the third), these are just the worlds how you'll experience them in the final game. Coincidentally these are the worlds you've already seen in previous demos, so don't get too excited now. The levels are as follows, when all 10 levels are complete and the extra shit that makes the levels playable is done, then the demo will be done.

World 1:
  • Little Isle, Level 1 - Good Luck Shore - 70%'ish complete, needs art (background, level decals, weather balloon exit anim) & tutorials
  • Little Isle, Level 2 - Friend of the Rocks - 60'ish complete, needs art (tilesets, level decals) & tutorials
  • Little Isle, Level 3 - Tip Man's Shack - 20% complete, art is complete, needs level design (rocket jump tutorial level, no enemies) & tutorials, also tip man himself (npc you can talk to for tips & level intro cutscene with him)
  • Little Isle, Level 4 (Optional Level) - The Great Above - 20% complete, optional speed level that tests rocket jump movement, can be accessed from level 2&3-- art is done but needs level design
  • Little Isle, Level 5 - Downpour Docks - 80'ish complete, finish "regretsman" boss & additional level decals

World 3:
  • Stone Zone, Level 1 - White Desert - 100% complete
  • Stone Zone, Level 2 - Whitest Desert - 100% complete
  • Stone Zone, Level 3 (Optional Level) - Storm Warning - 60%'ish complete, art is done & level is mostly complete, needs level design to be finished
  • Stone Zone, Level 4 - Concrete Star - 80'ish complete, start work on "boron fiend" boss & additional level decals, weather balloon exit anim wanted
  • Stone Zone, Level 5 - Fortress of Forget - 0% complete, needs tileset/level design/backgrounds/background switch feature/regretsman refight (powered up)
Misc Things:
  • World Select - 10%'ish complete, menu needs to be made but the pieces are there and mockups have been made for how it should look/feel
  • World Map - 50%'ish complete, currently can load levels & play levels but is missing level unlock features
  • Level Asset - 0% complete, "Grabbable Air Orbs", similar to Mischief Makers, forces the player to grab onto things and interact with the throwing system to get off of them
  • Level Asset - 0% complete, "Door", acts like the current portals but can only be used by grandman when grounded and by pressing "up" on them, 1-way
  • Level Outros - 0% complete, Exit Animations for a few levels that make it feel more connected (weather balloon exit anim)
When will all of this be done? I don't really know, but I'm working on it actively and hope to be done sooner rather than later. How far off is the final game? Fuck if I know.
